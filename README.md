FoodBridge AI
Overview
FoodBridge AI is an AI-powered food redistribution platform that connects restaurants, hotels, hostels, and event organizers with NGOs, shelters, and community kitchens.

Problem Statement
Large amounts of edible food are wasted daily while many communities face food insecurity. Existing donation processes are often manual and inefficient.

Solution
FoodBridge AI uses an intelligent matching algorithm to connect food donors with the most suitable recipients based on demand, location, capacity, urgency, and pickup deadlines.
